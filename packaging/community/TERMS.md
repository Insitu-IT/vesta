# Vesta license terms

The **Vesta MMIT License** sets out the rights to use, study, modify and redistribute Vesta Community. The **Eligibility Schedule** sets the rules for free use, free registration and paid licensing. Each product release identifies the applicable versions of both documents.

- [Vesta MMIT License 1.1](https://vestabackup.com/docs/legal/terms/mmit-1.1/)
- [Community Eligibility Schedule 2](https://vestabackup.com/docs/legal/eligibility/community-usage-v2/)
- [Registration privacy notice](https://vestabackup.com/docs/legal/privacy/vesta-registration-privacy-1.0/)

Read the [licensing guide](../../ELIGIBILITY.md) for practical examples. The [release tag](https://github.com/Insitu-IT/vesta/tags) and the license files shipped with your installation identify the terms for your copy.
