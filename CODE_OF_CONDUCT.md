# Code of Conduct

Treat people with respect. Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
Give actionable feedback and make room for newcomers. Harassment, threats,
personal attacks, discrimination and publishing someone's private information
are unacceptable in Vesta project spaces.

Use the hosting platform's private reporting tools for abuse or threats.
Maintainers may remove harmful material, limit participation or refer reports
to the platform as appropriate. Discuss moderation without exposing the
people involved or private report details.
