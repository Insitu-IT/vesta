Vesta registration privacy notice, version 1.0

Who operates the service

S2 Tikshuv Ltd, Israel, operates the Vesta website and licensing portal. Use the company contact at info@insitu.im with the subject “Vesta privacy” to request the appropriate private contact. Please include a return address and a brief description of your request. Keep one-time codes, certificates, customer data and recovery keys out of the initial message.

Reading the website

You can read the manuals and download the available API descriptions without an account. The web edge handles the IP address, request time, method, URL path, status and duration needed to serve the request and operate the site. The sign-in page remembers your language choice in your browser. An authenticated portal session uses a secure, HTTP-only first-party cookie. The portal allows ten minutes to complete a sign-in transaction. Its session lasts one hour and can be ended earlier by signing out. The identity provider expires an unused email code after five minutes and its authorization code after one minute.

The website uses S2's Elastic performance monitoring to diagnose page loading and errors. The website sends a limited description of the page and its performance to S2's monitoring endpoint. Vesta's installed backup application and its protected workloads have separate data flows. Your installation handles VM contents, backup objects and recovery keys in its configured environment.

Signing in and requesting a license

When you sign in, Vesta's identity service uses your email address to send a one-time code through Amazon Simple Email Service. The portal receives the verified email and the identity information needed to associate your requests with your account. A signed-in user can access their own requests and available certificates.

For a registration, we collect the installation identifier, the release, the organization name where applicable, the declared end use and revenue category, and confirmation of the presented terms and privacy notice. The form evaluates the usage figures you enter. Its durable declaration records the threshold comparisons. The issuer records the submission time, the document versions and digests, its outcome and any certificate it issues.

For a commercial request we also record the modules you request. When an authorized operator fulfills it, the record includes the approval and agreement reference. We use these records to handle your inquiry, establish the permission for the installation, supply the certificate, and resolve support or licensing disputes. We use the contact details collected here for licensing and service communications.

The certificate uses an installation identifier and a pseudonymous customer identifier. You may transfer the signed file to a disconnected installation, where Vesta verifies it locally. The production signing key stays in S2's issuer custody.

Where information goes

S2 uses AWS infrastructure in Stockholm for the Vesta portal. The configured mail relay is Amazon SES in Ireland. S2 operates the identity, issuer and monitoring systems. Access to the issuer store is limited to the licensing service and authorized operators. The web tier provides access to the account's results through a private connection to that service. Production host storage is encrypted and subject to the estate's backup policy.

Purposes and legal bases

Where the relevant law requires a legal basis, we process account details and requests to take steps you request and to provide the licensing service under the applicable agreement. We process limited operational records for our legitimate interests in protecting the service, diagnosing errors and responding to support requests. We retain records needed to meet applicable legal obligations and to establish or defend claims. Acknowledging this notice records which version you received. Any future optional use that requires consent needs a separate choice.

Retention and your choices

The licensing database keeps submitted declarations, their consent receipts, issued certificates and submitted commercial inquiries indefinitely by default. This includes inquiries that never result in an agreement and declarations after a certificate expires, is renewed or its license ends. The record retains the contact details and declared end use described above so S2 can identify the request, the permission granted and its history. There is no scheduled deletion of these licensing records. Identity-service audit logs, website access logs, monitoring events and backup rotation have their own retention arrangements.

The site needs a verified email and the registration fields to issue a certificate. You can continue to read the public manuals without registering. To ask for access, correction, restriction or deletion of your personal information, use the Vesta privacy contact. We may need to verify the request and assess which information remains necessary to establish a license, meet an applicable obligation or defend a claim. We will erase or restrict personal information where applicable law requires it. Depending on your jurisdiction you may also contact the relevant supervisory authority.

You can also request a copy of your information, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and request portability where applicable. We version this notice and the eligibility terms. A new registration records the documents you acknowledged at that time.
