Vesta Community Eligibility Schedule

Schedule community-usage-v2, version 2, accompanies MMIT 1.1. It takes effect for a copy of Vesta Community when S2 Tikshuv Ltd expressly distributes that copy under these terms and identifies this Schedule as applicable to that release. The scope and third-party notices shipped with the copy identify the covered material. S2's written adoption record identifies the effective date and covered release. For formal notices, contact S2 Tikshuv Ltd, Israel, at info@insitu.im with the subject “Vesta licensing”.

Personal use, internal use and revenue

Personal use means an individual's own private, nonbusiness activity. Eligible internal use means a company's operation for itself or its control group whose consolidated annual revenue is strictly below USD 20,000,000. The control group includes entities directly or indirectly controlling, controlled by, or under common control with the company. Control means holding more than half the voting rights or having power to direct management.

Use the latest completed financial year, with intragroup transactions eliminated. Use the USD figure in the ordinary consolidated accounts, or the documented exchange-rate basis used consistently to prepare those accounts. Reassess when new annual accounts are available or control changes. A new company without a completed financial year, or an organization whose legal category or accounting basis is uncertain, can request a written determination through the portal. Employees and contractors acting solely under an eligible company's operational control use that company's permission.

At USD 20 million or above, internal operation requires commercial permission. Managed-service operation for a third party requires a paid license from its first use. Separately supplied commercial modules require their own grant. A party outside the free categories can request a written evaluation agreement before it operates the software. A change to paid use requires appropriate permission from the change date.

Free registration for one installation

An otherwise eligible installation needs free registration when it has more than 100 successful backups and either more than 20 managed VMs or more than 2 registered workload agents. The comparisons are strict. At 101 backups, 20 VMs and two agents, the installation is below the trigger. With the same 101 backups, a 21st VM or a third agent triggers free registration. When the installation is below the trigger, otherwise eligible internal or personal use may continue without a license file.

Each installation has its own UUID. Count each distinct locally accepted successful backup once, including accepted backup points subsequently removed by retention. Retries of the same backup, unaccepted catalog imports and the S3 import placeholder contribute no additional count. Use the current managed VM inventory and registered workload-agent count for that installation at the time of assessment. Group revenue and managed-service status apply across the relevant organization. Moving or splitting an installation for the purpose of misrepresenting eligibility does not change the obligation to make a truthful declaration.

Declaration, renewal and disconnected sites

Submit when the installation first crosses the trigger. Use the Vesta licensing portal and verify the contact email with a one-time code. Enter the installation UUID, organization where applicable, end use, revenue category and local usage. Affirm the declaration, accept the identified MMIT and Eligibility Schedule and acknowledge the linked privacy notice. The issuer records the receipt and supplies a signed license file. A connected representative can transfer that license file to the installation and import it under Settings → Licensing using the release's separately verified issuer trust manifest. If the portal cannot be reached, contact info@insitu.im for a private submission route. During a service outage, retain a dated declaration and submit it within 30 days after service is restored.

A signed free license file covers one installation. At initial registration it expires on the second UTC calendar anniversary of issuance. A 29 February anniversary falls on 28 February in a year without a leap day. If the registration trigger still applies, confirm the current declaration for the same installation and fixed Schedule and import the new signed license file. Renewal opens 30 days before expiry. Early renewal extends the term by two calendar years from the existing expiry. Following lapse the two-year term begins at issuance. Eligibility changes take effect under the rules above. A renewed declaration records the contact and document versions for that submission. The installed product continues backup and recovery after the license file expires.

Notice, cure and recovery

Notify S2 promptly of a material error or change in a declaration. A remediable breach within an otherwise eligible grant receives the 30-day written-notice cure procedure in section 7 of the MMIT. Reinstatement after termination requires written confirmation from S2. Following the end of a prior permission, S2 may agree in writing to a recovery-only transition of up to 30 days for already protected data. Mandatory rights remain available. New backup services for third parties require their applicable commercial permission.

S2 handles licensing records under the linked registration privacy notice. Third-party material retains its original terms. Separately supplied commercial modules are covered by their own written grant.
