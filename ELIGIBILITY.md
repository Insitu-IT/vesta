# Which license do I need?

Vesta Community is free for personal use and for eligible internal use at organizations with consolidated annual revenue below USD 20 million.

| Your use | What to do |
|---|---|
| Personal or eligible internal use below the usage trigger | Start using Vesta. No registration is required. |
| Eligible internal or personal use above the trigger | Request a free two-year license for that installation. |
| Internal use at an organization with consolidated annual revenue of USD 20 million or more | Arrange a paid license before use. |
| Providing backup or recovery as a managed service for a third party | Arrange a paid license before first use, at any revenue or usage level. |

Paid pricing is by quote through the [licensing portal](https://vestabackup.com/portal/licensing).

An installation crosses the registration trigger after **more than 100 successful backups** while also having **more than 20 managed VMs or more than 2 registered agents**. For example, an eligible installation with 21 VMs and 100 successful backups can continue without registration. Its next successful backup triggers free registration.

Your ability to recover must never depend on your license status. Vesta shows licensing reminders in the console. Backup, scheduling and restore stay available during renewal and while an installation is disconnected.
